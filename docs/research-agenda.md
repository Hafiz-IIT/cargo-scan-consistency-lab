# Research agenda

## Central question
**How should declared cargo attributes be compared with imperfect physical observations before an automated system recommends inspection?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Field-specific tolerances reduce false alarms versus exact matching.

### H2
Weight and seal discrepancies should carry different operational severity than minor dimension drift.

### H3
Observation noise can create unacceptable review burden unless tolerances are calibrated.

## Experiments
1. Sweep measurement noise and tolerance thresholds over synthetic shipments.
2. Introduce single-field vs multi-field anomalies and compare severity policies.
3. Model biased sensors and study false-negative/false-positive tradeoffs.

## Metrics
- discrepancy recall
- false-review rate
- severity calibration
- review burden
- threshold sensitivity
